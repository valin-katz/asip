The ASIP Academic Model

Six Semesters. Engineered for Tangible Impact.

At Atria Institute of Technology, ASIP (Atria Societal Impact Projects) is a mandatory, credit-bearing 6-semester engineering sequence starting in the 3rd semester. Students formulate problem statements with real stakeholders and build validated, deployable hardware and software solutions.
